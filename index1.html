Smart India Hackathon (SIH) Problem & Solution Architecture

1. Problem Statement & Context

The North Eastern Region (NER) consists of 8 states connected only by the 22km-wide Siliguri Corridor. Topographical vulnerability (Himalayan young fold mountains, torrential monsoon rainfall, Brahmaputra basin floods) regularly severs lifelines like NH-6 (Sonapur Tunnel) and NH-27 (Dima Hasao ghats). A single landslide causes acute shortages of emergency medicines, fuel, and food grains across Tripura, Mizoram, and southern Assam within 48-72 hours.

SIH MVP Focus (Built & Working Here)
  • Interactive Leaflet GIS map with 8-state NER corridor overlays
  • AI route risk scoring (GSI landslide + IMD rainfall features)
  • Dynamic risk-adjusted A* safe alternate rerouting
  • Real-time GPS vehicle tracking simulation with AIS-140 style telemetry
  • PWA offline field incident reporting with IndexedDB queue & auto-sync
  • Automated alert engine with sound alerts & multilingual i18n
Full Enterprise Product Roadmap
  • Drone reconnaissance imagery & computer vision debris estimation
  • PostGIS spatial cluster partitioning across all 120 NER districts
  • Multi-modal routing: Inland Waterway NW-2 + Rail + Cargo Drones
  • Direct integration with MoRTH Bhoomi Rashi & CDAC National SMS Gateway
  • LoRaWAN & BLE peer-to-peer convoy mesh in deep mountain shadow zones

2. Explainable AI (XAI) Formula

Risk_Score = (Rain_48h * 0.28) + (Rain_1h * 0.18) + (Slope_deg * 0.20) + (Hist_Slides * 0.15) + (Soil_Sat * 0.10) + (River_Dist * 0.05) + (Incidents * 0.04)

SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) values decompose the risk score into clear percentages so that state disaster authorities immediately comprehend why an evacuation or road closure is warranted.
